# NoMAD attribution

Kerbside adapts the Kerberos authentication, password-change, credential-cache,
and legacy keychain patterns from [NoMAD](https://github.com/jamf/NoMAD),
`Universal` branch, commit `5471a31ec431701d5a74ea70a7e9754142bd8d6d`.
The upstream files are `NoMAD/KerbUtil.m`, `NoMAD/KlistUtil.swift`,
`NoMAD/NoMADUser.swift`, and `NoMAD/LDAPUtil.swift`. The account settings,
monitoring, and UI are a new small implementation.
